# What's new

What changed for you in each version of the Zulip elf, newest first. The desk shows the parts that are new when it offers you an
update.

## 1.6.0

- Digests and suggested replies ask the coding agent you choose: Claude Code, Codex or pi, under **Coding agent** in its settings (the runner's choice unless you pick one). Each is asked with no tools. It needs runner 0.43.0; with an older runner it asks Claude Code as before. Find out still uses Claude Code.

## 1.5.0

- Find out in my repos: no need to type a repo first. Claude picks the one of your repos likeliest to answer the
  [brackets], and the agent looks there. Your repos are the GitHub elf's Repos I maintain.
- The repo box suggests your repos as you type.

## 1.4.0

- Find out: when a suggested reply leaves something open, an agent looks it up for you. Needs runner 0.5.7 or newer.

## 1.3.3

- Reply sits next to Done, at the right of the topic's line.

## 1.3.2

- Behind the scenes: the conversation goes to Claude in a clearer form, with your own messages marked.

## 1.3.1

- Fixed: your messages in a conversation are recognised by your account, not your name.

## 1.3.0

- Suggested replies sound like you: set How you write in its settings.

## 1.2.0

- Reply from the tab: Claude suggests a reply, you edit it, and Send asks you first.

## 1.1.0

- See what Claude cost for each run, and this month's total.

## 1.0.0

- Sums up what was said in your Zulip streams and DMs, per topic, in its own tab.
